E-Sys started by OpenLauncher has an **OpenLauncher** section at the bottom of its left navigation bar. It's only in
E-Sys started by OpenLauncher, and needs E-Sys 25.x's navigation bar.

- [Compare NCD](@/tools/compare-ncd.md): two NCD files side by side, with names and value labels
- [Cheat Sheet](@/tools/cheat-sheet.md): E-Sys's FDL Editor next to every cheat sheet entry for the open CAFD
- [FA / SVT auto-backup](@/tools/fa-svt-backup.md): a copy of every FA and SVT read from the car
- [FA Helper](@/tools/fa-helper/index.md) (v3): vehicle order editor, checked against the type key, conflicts
  and your psdzdata's CAFDs
- [DTC](@/tools/dtc/index.md) (v3): read the car's fault codes, and clear them after coding
